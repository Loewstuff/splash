## Exact requirements

- **Overall vibe**: Clean, professional but light-hearted and funny. Professional yet approachable and playful. Excellent typography, generous but elegant spacing, strong contrast, warm and friendly without being childish or cluttered.
- **Hero image**: Large, prominent, centered photorealistic photo of a very cute, happy golden retriever with a friendly expression (high quality, well-lit, approachable).
- **Headline**: Large, bold, "Coming Soon"
- **Tagline** (use exactly): "Pawsitively fetching something awesome..."
- **Design direction**: Modern minimalist with warm gold/yellow accents. Soft warm gradient background (creams, warm off-whites, gentle golds). Subtle, tasteful animation on load (fade + gentle rise, respect prefers-reduced-motion).
- **Fully responsive**: Must look excellent on mobile (starting at ~320px) and desktop. Use modern responsive techniques (clamp for type, flexible layout, proper media queries).

- 100% single file. Everything embedded.
